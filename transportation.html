Affordable Transportation Options

Public Transit

Using buses, subways, and trains is often the most cost-effective way to get around campus and the city. Check your local transit authority’s website for routes and schedules.

Student Transit Passes

Many cities and universities offer discounted or free transit passes for students. Ask your school’s transportation office or student services for details on how to get yours.

Ride-Sharing and Carpooling

Services like Uber and Lyft may offer student discounts or promotions. Carpooling with classmates can save money and reduce your carbon footprint.

Bike Rentals and Sharing Programs

Many campuses and cities have bike rental or bike-share programs. They’re a great way to get exercise and avoid transit costs. Some programs offer special rates for students.

Tips for Saving on Transportation

  • Plan your trips ahead to avoid last-minute expenses.
  • Use transit apps to track bus/train times.
  • Consider walking or biking for short distances.
  • Check if your campus has free shuttle services.
