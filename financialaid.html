Financial Aid for College Students

Scholarships

Scholarships are a great way to fund your education without paying it back. Look for scholarships based on merit, field of study, background, or interests. Use websites like Fastweb and College Board’s Scholarship Search.

Grants

Grants are typically need-based financial aid you don’t have to repay. Check federal, state, and institutional grants. Examples include Pell Grants and state-specific aid programs.

FAFSA Tips

The Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) is essential for accessing many types of aid. File early to maximize your chances. Keep your tax info handy, and double-check your application for accuracy.

Loans

Student loans can help bridge gaps but be careful with borrowing. Federal loans usually have lower interest rates and flexible repayment. Understand your loan terms and borrow only what you need.
