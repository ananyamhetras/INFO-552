Eating Well on a Student Budget

Cheap Meal Prep Ideas

Preparing your meals in advance can save both money and time. Try simple recipes like pasta, stir-fry vegetables with rice, or overnight oats for breakfast. Use ingredients that can be bought in bulk and stored.

Affordable Grocery Stores

Look for local discount grocery stores, dollar stores, or bulk food outlets. Farmers markets sometimes offer fresh produce at lower prices, especially near closing time.

Campus Dining Hacks

Many campuses offer meal plans with different tiers. Compare costs carefully and use dining dollars before they expire. Some cafeterias offer discounts during off-peak hours.

Local Affordable Restaurants

Explore nearby food trucks, ethnic restaurants, and student-friendly cafes. Many offer daily specials or student discounts—always ask!
