Affordable Housing for College Students

On-Campus vs. Off-Campus Housing

Living on-campus can be convenient and sometimes more affordable, especially for freshmen. However, off-campus options may offer more independence and potentially lower costs if you find roommates.

Finding Roommates

Sharing housing costs with roommates is a popular way to reduce rent. Try student Facebook groups, campus bulletin boards, or roommate matching websites specific to your college.

Affordable Neighborhoods

Research neighborhoods near campus that offer lower rent prices and easy access to public transportation. Websites like Craigslist, Zillow, or local rental listing boards can help.
