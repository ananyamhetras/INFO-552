Finding Part-Time Work as a Student

On-Campus Jobs

Many colleges offer on-campus positions like library assistant, lab helper, or campus ambassador. These jobs are convenient and often flexible around your class schedule.

Work-Study Opportunities

If you qualify for federal work-study, check your school’s job board for eligible positions. These roles usually pay fairly and provide valuable work experience.

Freelancing Ideas

Consider freelance work such as tutoring, graphic design, writing, or social media management. Websites like Upwork and Fiverr can help you find gigs.

Resume Resources

Your school’s career center can assist with resume writing, interview prep, and job search strategies. Make sure your resume highlights relevant skills and experiences.
